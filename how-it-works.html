How LipaLink Works

Simple, transparent installment purchasing for buyers and effortless collection tracking for sellers.

1

Find a Product or Create an Agreement

Select products directly from verified online shops on our marketplace, or create a private pay-small installment plan with your offline merchant using their LipaLink store ID.

2

Pay Flexible Installments

Make micro-payments daily, weekly, or monthly using Mobile Money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money) or Direct Bank Transfers at your own comfortable pace.

3

Track Progress & Instant Receipts

Both buyer and seller receive instant SMS notifications, digital receipt proofs, and automated balance updates after every single payment made.
