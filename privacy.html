Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you use LipaLink. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, phone number, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Store details (sellers): business name, location, category, owner name and payout preference.
  • Agreement and payment records: the products or services you buy or sell, agreed prices, installment schedules, payment confirmations and receipts.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, IP address and basic usage logs needed to keep the Platform secure and reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and store.
  • To record installment agreements and track payments between buyers and sellers.
  • To send payment reminders, receipts, agreement updates and security alerts.
  • To verify sellers, detect fraud and resolve disputes.
  • To improve the Platform and, where you opt in, to tell you about new shops and offers.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only when needed to run the service:

  • The other party to your agreement. A seller sees the buyer's name, phone number and payment progress; a buyer sees the seller's business details.
  • Payment providers (Mobile Money operators and banks) to process payments and payouts.
  • Messaging providers to deliver SMS, email and WhatsApp notifications.
  • Authorities where required by Tanzanian law or to protect users from fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Payment data

LipaLink never stores your Mobile Money PIN or full bank card details. Payments are confirmed by the payment provider; we keep the transaction reference, amount, method and status so that we can issue receipts and track your installment plan.

5. SMS, email and WhatsApp

We send transactional messages (reminders, receipts, agreement updates) to the phone number and email on your account. You can switch optional channels, such as WhatsApp updates and marketing emails, on or off from the Settings page at any time.

6. How we protect your information

  • Passwords are hashed with industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text.
  • Connections to the Platform are encrypted with TLS.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to support you.
  • Session cookies are marked HttpOnly and SameSite to reduce the risk of hijacking.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Agreement and payment records are retained for as long as required for accounting, tax and dispute-resolution purposes under Tanzanian law, even after an account is closed.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you may:

  • Access and update your profile details from the My Profile page.
  • Change notification preferences from the Settings page.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Close your account from the Settings page, after settling any active installment plans.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and small amounts of browser storage to remember preferences such as your language. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

10. Children

LipaLink is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Platform evolves. We will notify registered users of material changes by email or in-app notice.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to privacy@lipalink.co.tz.
