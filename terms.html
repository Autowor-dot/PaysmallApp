Unless expressly stated otherwise, LipaLink is not the seller of listed goods or services and is not a party to the installment agreement between a buyer and a seller.

4. Buyer obligations

  • Pay each installment on or before its due date as set out in the agreed payment schedule.
  • Understand that ownership of a product normally transfers only after the agreed release condition (for example full payment) is met.
  • Do not create agreements with the intention of not paying. Persistent non-payment may lead to account suspension and the seller may pursue recovery under Tanzanian law.

5. Seller obligations

  • List only goods and services you are legally entitled to sell, described accurately and priced transparently.
  • Honour the delivery or service conditions in every agreement you accept.
  • Complete business verification when requested and keep payout details up to date.
  • Do not charge buyers fees or interest that were not disclosed in the agreement.

6. Installment agreements

An agreement created through the Platform becomes binding when the seller accepts it. The agreement records the total price, initial deposit, installment schedule, delivery conditions, grace period and any late-payment, cancellation or refund policy. Both parties receive a copy and can view it in their LipaLink account at any time.

Changes to an accepted agreement require consent from both buyer and seller through the Platform.

7. Payments and receipts

  • Payments are made through supported Mobile Money services (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa) or bank transfer.
  • Every confirmed payment generates a digital receipt that is stored in your account and sent by SMS or email.
  • A payment is only considered complete once the payment provider confirms it. Pending payments may take time to settle.
  • Payouts to sellers are routed to the wallet or bank account they registered.
