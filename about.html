Buying on installments, done the fair way.

LipaLink (PaySmallApp) is a Tanzanian installment-payment platform. Buyers get the phones, appliances, furniture and tools they need by paying small by small; sellers get a clear agreement, automatic reminders and every shilling tracked.

Why we built LipaLink

In markets like Kariakoo, "lipa kidogo kidogo" has always existed, but it ran on trust, notebooks and WhatsApp messages. Buyers lost track of what they owed; sellers chased payments and lost sales to customers who could not pay in full. LipaLink puts a written agreement, a payment schedule and digital receipts behind every deal, so both sides know exactly where they stand.

What we stand for

Three promises guide every feature we build.

Fair for both sides

The agreement records the price, deposit, schedule and delivery terms before any money moves, and neither party can change it alone.

Every payment counted

Each installment gets a digital receipt and updates the remaining balance instantly, whether paid by Mobile Money, bank or cash recorded by the seller.

Safe by design

Verified seller profiles, protected accounts, suspicious-activity monitoring and a support team that reviews disputes against the recorded facts.

How a deal works

Find the item

Browse listings from LipaLink sellers or search the online catalog, or bring any deal you made offline.

Agree the plan

Set the deposit, installment amount, frequency and when the goods are released. The seller accepts and both receive the agreement.

Pay small by small

Pay each installment from your dashboard with Mobile Money or bank transfer. Reminders arrive before each due date.

Own it

When the plan completes, the seller releases the item and the final receipt closes the agreement.
